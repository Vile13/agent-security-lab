---
title: Hardware Refresh Policy
source: intranet/hr/hardware-refresh
trust: internal
---
Company laptops are replaced on a 36 month cycle. Replacement is initiated
automatically by IT Asset Management; employees do not need to file a request.

Devices returned at the end of a cycle are wiped to NIST SP 800-88 Purge level
before being resold or recycled. Employees who need a device earlier than the
36 month mark because of a hardware fault should open a support ticket.

Contractors and interns are issued devices from the loan pool and are outside
the refresh cycle.
